# Full Story Guidelines

## Authority and structure

- Write only in polished, idiomatic English.
- Treat the premise as a non-exhaustive creative foundation and use the validated Story Segment Core (`SSC`) as the required narrative structure.
- Every explicit premise fact must remain true. Preserve SSC facts, chronology, scene IDs, segment IDs and continuity, and keep the finished Story recognizably grounded in the premise's core idea.
- Write 500–1000 words in total across all segment prose fields. Target `500 + 5 × premise word count`, capped at 1000 words when the premise contains 100 words or more; titles, headings and IDs are outside the count.
- Present segments in SSC order and preserve their semantic scene grouping.
- Give every segment narrative movement from its START state through MID to END while remaining coherent with both inter-keyframe intervals.
- Within a scene, the next segment must begin from the same semantic state in which the preceding segment ended.
- Between scenes, preserve any deliberate cut, location change or time jump specified by SSC.

## Prose quality

- Develop a complete chronological Story with concrete causality, motivation, setting, atmosphere and visible action.
- Freely add characters, locations, events, motivations, transitions, sensory detail and narrative texture needed for a complete and compelling Story when each addition is compatible with every explicit premise fact.
- Use broad creative autonomy for a sparse premise. As premise detail increases, prefer developing its supplied material over unnecessary invention.
- Add nuance through precise, meaningful detail rather than ornamental filler.
- Maintain consistent tense, point of view, identity, appearance, objects, environment and chronology.
- Do not add arbitrary conflict, spectacle or plot turns merely to increase length.
- Retain useful non-visual narrative context while keeping physical events concrete enough for later visual production.

## Output boundary

- Output labeled scene and segment sections followed only by Story prose.
- Do not include workflow prompts, analysis, QC commentary, production instructions or unresolved placeholders.
- Full is one required artifact of the same Story generation that also produces Prompt-Realized; it is never a separate user-requested run.
